    Help

    What is rbx2017?

    A fan-run server where you play on the 2017 version of the Roblox client.

    Do I need a Roblox account?

    No. Sign up here or in the launcher, or play as a guest. It's separate from Roblox, so use a different password.

    What do I need to play?

    A Windows PC. On Windows 7, 8 or 8.1, get the Windows 7 version from the Download page. Mac, Linux and phones aren't supported.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Game passes and in-game items cost R$, which you earn by playing. R$ can't be bought.

    How do I get R$?

    New accounts start with R$ 200. You also get R$ 25 a day for opening the launcher or this site, R$ 5 for every 10 minutes in a game (up to R$ 60 a day), and R$ 10 for each badge. Your balance is next to your name in the launcher and here once you log in.

    Windows warns me about the installer

    The launcher isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows doesn't recognize it yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    The launcher's code is on GitHub.

    How do I update?

    You don't need to. The launcher updates itself when it starts.
